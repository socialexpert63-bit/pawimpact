Questions

Is an adoption inquiry the same as an adoption?

No. Only the shelter can confirm an adoption, and PawImpact reports them separately.

Are pledges counted as funds received?

No. Proposed, signed, invoiced and received amounts are tracked separately.

Does the AI publish on its own?

Never. Every draft is editable and needs human approval.

How are photos and youth data handled?

Consent is required before publishing identifiable photos or videos, and participant records are limited to authorized adults.
